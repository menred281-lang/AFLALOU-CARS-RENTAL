How this version works

01

About AFLALOU Cars

AFLALOU Cars is an offline car-catalogue and travel-budget planning app associated with AFLALOU CARS RENTAL. It is intended for adults. The catalogue uses fictional sample cars, static AI-generated illustrations and illustrative rates. Images are not photographs of the company fleet. The app does not confirm availability, make reservations, accept payments or check driving eligibility. No AI generation takes place in the app.

02

No personal data collected or shared

The app does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data with the developer or third parties. It has no accounts, booking forms, document uploads, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. It does not access your identity, contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or personal files, and does not read advertising IDs or other device identifiers. It makes no network requests and requests no Android permissions. Android app backup is disabled.

03

Your choices stay in temporary memory

Search, rental dates, vehicle choices, favourites, comparison selections, rate/seat filters, optional extras, fuel and shared-cost figures, and checklist answers are processed locally in app memory. They are not sent to a server or deliberately saved as persistent preferences. Temporary choices can remain while the app is backgrounded. Reset session in About clears the main planning state, including vehicle-inspection checks. Fuel and shared-cost inputs are discarded when you leave their screens. Closing the app process clears temporary state. No account or server record exists to delete. Clearing app state is not a promise of forensic memory erasure.

04

Your device and other services

The logo and car pictures are bundled with the app. No map, email, payment, social-media or AI service is integrated. The app disables personalised keyboard learning for its input fields where supported, but cannot control your keyboard or operating system. Android, Google Play, your device manufacturer and your keyboard provider may independently handle installation, system, purchase or diagnostic information under their own settings and policies. The app does not receive that information. Since it sends no user data, it does not provide a data-transmission encryption service or claim an independent security certification.
